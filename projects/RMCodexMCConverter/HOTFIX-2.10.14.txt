RMCodexMCConverter 2.10.14 - 2026-09-12

Resource-only datapack and repair-handoff hardening:

- Class-free Modrinth wrapper JARs now follow an explicit datapack conversion route.
- Universal wrapper metadata prefers NeoForge and supports single-quoted inline TOML fields.
- Datapacks emit a NeoForge 26.2 lowcodefml wrapper with showAsDataPack=true and pack format 107.
- Fabric/Quilt descriptors are removed from the NeoForge target.
- Asset recovery no longer imports resources from arbitrary sibling JARs.
- Partial conversion failures offer KAT repair even before a full scaffold exists.
- Verified against Wild Animals 1.5 with a clean Gradle build and no foreign assets.
